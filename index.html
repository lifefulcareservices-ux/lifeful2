Welcome to Lifeful

Leicester’s Trusted Dementia Day Care Centre

At Lifeful, we provide a supportive and uplifting environment where individuals living with dementia can enjoy their day, build connections and feel a sense of purpose.

Our centre helps families and carers with daytime respite, knowing their loved ones are in safe hands and supported by a caring, professional team.

A typical day

A reassuring six-hour routine

Our schedule offers structure, social connection, stimulation and calm throughout the day.

9:30 AMArrival, welcome and settling in
9:30 AMMorning orientation and conversation
10:00 AMCognitive activity or themed group session
11:00 AMGentle movement or seated exercise
12:00 PMMidday break, refreshments and social time
1:00 PMQuiet time, sensory support or music
2:00 PMCreative, social or reminiscence activity
3:30 PMHome time and family handover

For families and carers

Respite with reassurance

We understand that supporting a loved one with dementia can be emotional and demanding. Lifeful provides daytime respite with a strong focus on communication, continuity and peace of mind.

  • Pre-attendance assessment and person-centred planning
  • Friendly handovers and regular communication
  • Dementia-friendly environment and consistent routines
  • Compassionate support from a trained team
